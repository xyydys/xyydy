AwemeLiquidBar 1.0.58 auth47 ClearGlassPolicy v11.1 compile fix

Fix only: cross-translation-unit C/C++ linkage for AWEAppResolvedAppearanceStyle.

GitHub/Xcode27 linker error:
  Undefined symbol: AWEAppResolvedAppearanceStyle(UIWindow*)
  NOTE: found _AWEAppResolvedAppearanceStyle in Tweak.x, declaration possibly missing extern "C"

Cause:
  Tweak.x exports AWEAppResolvedAppearanceStyle with C linkage.
  MessageTopGlassV3.xm is compiled as Objective-C++ by the Logos toolchain and the v11 declaration lacked extern "C", so the call site requested a C++-mangled symbol.

Resolution:
  Wrap the MessageTopGlassV3.xm declaration in __cplusplus and use extern "C" for Objective-C++ builds. No UI, policy, geometry, touch, or glass behavior changed from v11.
