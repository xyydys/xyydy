AwemeLiquidBar 1.0.56c12 — targeted c10 follow-up

BASE
- Built from 1.0.56c10 auth37 (not the unrelated 1.0.56c11 SWJ experiment).
- Preserves the 1.0.56c8 playback-lifecycle fix and 1.0.56c10 true ownership-release implementation.

TARGETED FIXES
1. Home article-style feed items cannot leave LiquidBar permanently hidden; visible Home feed chrome is authoritative unless an explicit route suppresses the bar.
2. IM composer routes suppress LiquidBar and opt out of internal fullscreen geometry, preventing bottom-bar leakage under sent images and avoiding forwarded-work UI being lifted by a second bottom footprint.
3. Search routes opt out of internal fullscreen geometry so tapping search-result works does not enter a competing feed-height rewrite path.
4. AWEFeedTableView fullscreen sizing only expands a native one-tab-short layout and never rewrites its frame while tracking/dragging/decelerating.
5. Top category visibleCount=1 now follows selectedIndex instead of always showing windowStart 0.
6. Authorization adds Hoeyc_0505: auth37 -> auth38.
7. True bottom-bar ownership-release setting is visible and usable only for the authorized account digests corresponding to Hoeyc_0505 and 0416xxd. All other accounts are forced to normal LiquidBar-visible behavior, including stale defaults.

NOT CHANGED
- No SWJ compatibility logic from 1.0.56c11.
- No forced play/pause changes.
- No chat interactive-return experiments.
- No settings-drawer geometry experiments.
- No process-wide UIView hook.
