AwemeLiquidBar 1.0.58 auth47 — Clear Glass Native v10
=====================================================

Goal
----
Retire the per-video luminance-adaptive experiment and keep UIKit as the owner
of Liquid Glass material/interaction wherever possible.

Core material changes
---------------------
1. iOS 27 Home buttons use clearGlassButtonConfiguration with no synthetic
   Light/Dark material pin and no per-video screenshot/luminance sampling.
2. iOS 27 Message Menu/Search/Add use the same Clear configuration and remain
   at Douyin's native source positions.
3. iOS 27 bottom bar follows DYKiller's floating UITabBar finding: the floating
   capsule material is changed on _UITabBarItemPlatterView.glassEffect rather
   than UITabBarAppearance.backgroundEffect. The existing interactive flag is
   preserved and repeated writes are skipped while our installed effect remains.
4. iOS 26 stays on native UIKit Regular/Clear configuration and follows the
   app/page appearance and system transparency behavior.

Interaction fixes
-----------------
- Right Like/Comment/Favorite/Share glass buttons are real interactive UIButtons;
  TouchUp is forwarded to the live AWEFeedVideoButton action path, preferring
  the same centre/lower hit regions where Douyin's numeric/count target works.
- Top Search is a real interactive glass UIButton. Douyin's private recognizer
  is left on its original owner; completed taps are forwarded programmatically.
  Bottom UISearchTab remains independent.
- Avatar is rendered as the content of the real glass UIButton and the UIMenu
  is attached directly to that same button. The old separate invisible menu hit
  target is disabled.
- Same-style/music base is a real interactive glass UIButton, mirrors the live
  original artwork into that UIButton, hides the duplicate source while bound,
  and forwards to the current bottom action owner. Material and artwork therefore
  participate in the same UIKit press/stretch state.
- Message top proxies remain true interactive native-glass UIButtons so press /
  stretch comes from UIKit rather than a synthetic scale animation.

Profile/Favourites scroll stability
-----------------------------------
Profile/Favourites routes no longer schedule global bottom-geometry refreshes
from setContentOffset, and bottom adjustments are skipped while the route's
scroll view is tracking/dragging/decelerating. This is intended to stop a small
pull-down after deep scrolling from resetting a nested profile pager to top.

Validation performed here
-------------------------
- scripts/preflight.sh passes, including Swift frontend parse and Logos
  structure/token checks.
- No full Theos/Xcode27 arm64/arm64e build is available in this environment;
  the GitHub builder remains the compile authority.

Primary device checks
---------------------
1. iOS 27: bottom capsule is visibly Clear and follows system transparency.
2. Direct taps on the four right-side glass icons open their actions (not only
   taps on the numeric labels below).
3. Top Search and bottom Search can both open Search independently.
4. Avatar/menu and same-style/music glass bases show native press/stretch and
   remain responsive.
5. iOS 27 Message top buttons are Clear rather than permanently light.
6. Deep-scroll Favourites/Profile, then slightly pull down: list must not jump
   to its first video.
