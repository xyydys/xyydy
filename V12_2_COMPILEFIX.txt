AwemeLiquidBar 1.0.58 auth47 InteractionWarp v12.2 compilefix

Scope: compile-only fix. No runtime behavior or feature policy changes.

GitHub/Xcode 27 error fixed:
  MessageTopGlassV3.xm: cannot jump from switch statement to this case label

Cause:
  AWEMessageGlassTouchDriver -handlePress: used Objective-C object `plate` in
  animation blocks while `switch (recognizer.state)` case labels shared one
  lexical scope. Under Objective-C++/ARC, Clang treats the strongly captured
  local as having a non-trivial lifetime and rejects case-label jumps across it.

Fix:
  Give Began, Changed, Ended/Cancelled/Failed, and default case bodies explicit
  brace scopes. This matches the already compiling pattern used by
  AWEGlassPressFeedbackTarget in Tweak.x and does not alter interaction logic.

Validation:
  scripts/preflight.sh passed.
  Touch-driver case-scope audit passed.
  MessageTopGlassV3.xm raw brace balance = 0.
