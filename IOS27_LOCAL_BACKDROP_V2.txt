iOS 27 local-backdrop adaptive glass v2

- Automatic mode no longer reads Douyin glyph tint/color.
- No global source-tint signal controls iOS 27 glass style.
- Each native glass button stays UIUserInterfaceStyleUnspecified and resolves its own backdrop.
- Automatic glyph contrast is derived from that button's resolved local glass material.
- Bottom bar auto mode no longer uses the corner probe to force Light/Dark; overlay/controller/tab bar remain Unspecified.
- Force Dark remains the only explicit iOS 27 style override.
